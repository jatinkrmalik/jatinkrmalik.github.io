the "atmanirbhar" lie // why 🇮🇳 startup ecosystem love dropshippers and hate engineers?

you can't make this stuff up anons. the absolute state of the indian tech ecosystem on full display.

Jatin Kumar Malik

>be me, browsing tech news

>see headlines about “india’s first ai summit” in delhi

>expectation: novel neural net architectures, homegrown llms, groundbreaking robotics ip.

>reality: galgotias university presents a unitree go2 (chinese robodog you can buy on alibaba for $2k) as their own “indigenous ai innovation” named “orion”.

>mfw

you can’t make this stuff up anons. the absolute state of the indian tech ecosystem on full display. the internet is losing its mind, but if you’ve been paying attention for more than 5 minutes, this is just business as usual.

the entire “startup” ecosystem there is built on one simple algo:

  1. find a cheap odm/oem product in shenzhen or europe.
  2. order 10k units with your logo slapped on it.
  3. run ads with saffron flags screaming “made in india” and “atmanirbhar” (self-reliant).
  4. ???
  5. profit.

the historical scrolls of arbitrage:

> level 1: the gadget flippers

micromax / lava: the og grifters. became #1 by dropshipping generic mediatek phones from coolpad. got vaporized the second chinese oems decided to sell directly. boat / noise: built billion-dollar empires reselling $5 shenzhen earbuds. zero ip. they don’t even own the plastic molds. just marketing companies masquerading as tech.

> level 2: the “premium” d2c scams

mokobara: “luxury” luggage brand caught selling generic taobao suitcases at a 500% markup. when people called them out, they literally released a discount code called “whitelabel”. you have to respect the absolute brazenness.

agaro / lifelong: the amazon kings. air fryers, foot massagers, treadmills. sounds italian or indian? nope. it’s all generic chinese e-waste rebadged from guangdong xinbao. they don’t have a factory, they have a warehouse and a guy who knows photoshop.

> level 3: the “tesla of india” illusion

ola electric: claims to have designed the revolutionary s1 pro scooter in bengaluru.

reality check: in 2020 they bought a dutch startup called etergo. look up the “etergo appscooter”. it is the exact same vehicle. they bought the ip, cancelled the swappable battery, and marketed it as 100% desi genius. it’s not “engineered in india”, it’s “acquired in amsterdam”.

> level 4: the ev subsidy loot

entire tier-2 cities flooded with “green energy” scooter brands. they import ckd kits (completely knocked down) from china. hire 5 guys with screwdrivers to assemble them in a shed. claim government fame-ii subsidies for “local manufacturing”.

battery catches fire in 3 months. company dissolves and rebrands.

why this destroys actual innovation:

imagine being a genuinely based indian engineer trying to build real tech.

you: “i need $5m for a 5-year r&d runway to develop novel silicon photonics or proprietary slam algorithms. high risk, insane technical barrier.”

desi vc: “hmm, sounds hard. show me monthly recurring revenue (mrr).”

chad rebrander: “bro, i need $2m to import a container of smart water bottles from guangzhou and run fb ads. 4x roas guaranteed in 6 months.”

desi vc: “shut up and take my money!”

this is why they will never have a tsmc, an nvidia, or a boston dynamics. real r&d is capex heavy but rebranding is opex heavy. investors there want quick flips and easy exits.

why fund a phd’s risky 5-year project when you can fund a marketing bro’s arbitrage scheme and flip it to a bigger fool in 2 years?

the robot dog wasn’t a mistake, it’s the perfect mascot for their entire ecosystem. a chinese product wearing an indian costume, barking for investor cash.

it’s over.
