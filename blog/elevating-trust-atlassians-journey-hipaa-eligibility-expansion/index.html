Elevating Trust: Atlassian's Journey to HIPAA Eligibility Expansion!

How Atlassian expanded HIPAA eligibility by replacing a manual BAA process with a self-serve flow and a new service integrating IronClad with Admin Hub.

Jatin Kumar Malik

Atlassian is deeply committed to earning the trust of our enterprise customers. As we scale our cloud platform, our investments are strategically aimed at reinforcing data governance, privacy, security, and compliance. These pillars are essential in our quest to not only meet but exceed the expectations of those we serve. Our dedication to these areas is what drives our continuous efforts to expand HIPAA eligibility, ensuring the utmost protection of PHI (Protected health information) data.

We are thrilled to announce that we have successfully expanded HIPAA eligibility, enabling thousands of our customers to manage their data within our platforms securely. 🎉

This post is more than a recount; it’s a narrative of innovation and dedication. You’ll discover our unique approach to problem-solving that blends technology with creativity, always with our customers’ needs at the forefront. Our ‘Don’t F*ck the Customer’ (DFTC) value is not just a guiding principle but the standard we hold ourselves to with every challenge we tackle!

As a Principal Engineer deeply involved in this project, I had the privilege of steering our efforts towards solutions that not only comply with regulations but also empower our customers with unmatched ease and confidence in managing sensitive data.

Join me as I take you through an inside look at how we turned complex compliance requirements into a seamless and secure experience for our users! 🔎

Understanding HIPAA and Our Commitment

What is HIPAA?

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) sets the standard for protecting sensitive patient data. Organizations dealing with protected health information (PHI) must ensure that the necessary security measures are in place and followed.

Generated via DALL·E 3

For HIPAA compliance, Atlassian has been following a Good - Better - Best model of iterative development. Our MVP release targeted customers in our cloud enterprise plan and only such customers could sign the Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with Atlassian. Additionally, to maintain compliance, email and push notifications had to be disabled.

We knew that we had to get better in our subsequent releases to expand our HIPAA eligibility! 🚀

Innovating HIPAA Compliance!

Our new release has allowed both Standard and Premium customers to sign the BAA agreements with Atlassian via a self-service flow. This flow, managed through the AdminHub interface, simplifies the process, especially for our larger Cloud Enterprise customers who face more intricate legal complexities.

Furthermore, our paid plan customers can now designate product instances that will store Protected Health Information (PHI) independently. This ensures the right controls are applied where necessary without affecting other instances.

Perhaps one of the most exciting features for Jira Software & Jira Service Management customers is the ability to enable email and push notifications. While these notifications will be redacted for the protection of PHI data, it’s a significant step forward in balancing compliance and functionality. 📈

Designing for Scalability and Efficiency

With our expansion of HIPAA eligibility, we needed a robust, scalable, and efficient architecture. Our goal was to eliminate unnecessary human interventions and make the process more seamless for our customers, regardless of their size.

Let’s delve into how we achieved this.

Identifying Bottlenecks

ℹ️ One of the important pillars of the HIPAA enablement process is our partnership with IronClad, our trusted underlying vendor and System of Record for all compliance-related workflows. IronClad’s robust platform has been instrumental in managing our Business Associate Agreements (BAAs), which are vital contracts that bind us and our customers to the stringent data protection standards set by HIPAA.

In the past, enterprise customers seeking to activate HIPAA capabilities on their products were required to engage with our customer advocate team. This initial step would activate our sales team to start the IronClad workflow within Salesforce. Customers had to undergo the critical step of signing a BAA, which not only formalized their commitment to HIPAA regulations but also solidified the trust between us and our users.

A rough overview of the initial process to enable a HIPAA customer

The process started with the sales team entering information to begin the IronClad workflow, leading to a BAA link being sent to customers. Customer Advocates would then poll the system to check BAA status and upon completion, the final agreement was stored manually in the object store.

Post-signature, our Customer Advocates would then mark the product as compliant in our systems via the internal admin panel for org-policy. Then, they would go on to disable push and email notifications for HIPAA-compliant products.

As you can observe, while this method served its purpose initially, it was operational-intensive and did not scale well with the increasing interest in HIPAA compliance among our expanding customer base! Acknowledging this bottleneck, we set out to innovate and automate, aiming to transform this manual workflow into a streamlined, scalable self-serve model.

Crafting a Future-Proof Compliance Architecture

We gathered a team of seasoned engineers and began a series of intensive brainstorming sessions, each of us bringing our expertise and experience to the table. Our mission was clear: to design a solution that was as elegant as it was functional, one that would seamlessly integrate with our existing systems and provide a robust foundation for the future.

We contemplated several paths forward:

❌ The first was to weave IronClad directly into our Org-Policy, allowing for a direct but complex interaction.

❌ Another was to empower our Admin Hub to communicate with IronClad, creating a more centralized control point.

✅ The third option, and ultimately the one we chose, was more ambitious: to build an entirely new service that would act as a conduit between IronClad and our Admin Hub!

As the team and I delved into the third option — developing a new service to bridge IronClad and our Admin Hub — we weighed its merits and potential drawbacks with meticulous care. This approach promised a greater degree of control over the intricacies of policy management, a pivotal aspect of maintaining HIPAA compliance.

Generated via DALL·E 3

The benefits were clear and compelling. With this new system, we would gain the ability to update the workflow status in our database in real-time, ensuring that our teams always had the latest information at their fingertips. Our auditing and troubleshooting capabilities would be greatly enhanced, providing us with sharper tools to maintain the rigour of our compliance environment. Furthermore, this solution offered us the flexibility to scale and evolve, adding new features and regulatory flows as the landscape changed.

However, we were acutely aware of the challenges ahead. Setting up webhooks — the very threads that would provide us with those real-time updates — presented a complexity that could not be understated. The creation of a new service entailed not only additional production costs but also demanded a comprehensive security review to uphold our unwavering commitment to data protection.

Every choice had its trade-offs, but in the end, we were confident that the advantages of this path would serve our customers best, positioning us to adapt swiftly to both their needs and the regulatory demands of tomorrow.

Designing Our Compliance Database

Generated via DALL·E 3

Central to the functionality of our new service was a meticulously designed database schema, crafted to support the creation, reading, and updating of agreement entries with precision

⌗ agreements

Our agreementstable became the repository of record, each entry a testament to an agreement forged with our customers, identifiable by a unique EntityId. This setup not only allowed for swift retrieval of agreement statuses to be displayed on our Admin Hub but also facilitated immediate updates through webhook triggers—each change meticulously logged with who made it, what was changed, and why! We kept the agreement schema generic enough to serve future use cases other than agreementType:HIPAA and workflowType:IRONCLAD ensuring we can use a single service regulatory-compliance-hub to scale.

⌗ agreements-audit-log

This table served as our historical ledger, capturing every alteration in an immutable log, ensuring full transparency and a robust trail for audits.

This dual-table architecture was not just about storing data; it was about creating a narrative for each agreement, fostering trust through traceability, and ensuring that as we scaled, our integrity scaled with us.

Seamless Integration with IronClad

Our service utilizes IronClad APIs, such as:

  • Create Workflow API: To initiate workflows.
  • Get Workflow Status API: To track the status of ongoing workflows.
  • Webhook Calls: To ensure real-time updates of workflow statuses.

❗️ A comprehensive security review was conducted to ensure the safety of both REST APIs and WEBHOOKS as it meant an external third-party system will be calling our internal service.

Simplifying the BAA Signing Experience

A very simplified version of the BAA Signing flow

As you can see, this approach leveraged the power of REST APIs and WebHooks, a setup that allowed us to monitor real-time changes and keep our workflow statuses up-to-date.

The webhooks acted as our sentinels, notifying us of changes as they happened and ensuring that our database was always up to date. When it came to the all-important BAAs, we opted for the security of an encrypted S3 bucket, where signed documents could rest easy, safe from prying eyes.

Streamlining Product HIPAA Tagging

A very simplified version of the Product HIPAA Tagging flow

A potential HIPAA user’s tagging interaction begins when post BAA signing they land on AdminHub. Meanwhile, behind the scenes, IronClad asynchronously sends the BAA status updates via webhooks This information is then utilized by the Admin Hub within Atlassian’s infrastructure, which checks the BAA status when a page is loaded.

If the BAA is confirmed as signed by our service’s DB, the Admin Hub allows the action to tag the HIPAA policy, thereby ensuring that the user’s interaction complies with HIPAA regulations. Upon positive verification, it initiates an upsert action for the HIPAA policy in the org-policy database. This process ensures that the organization’s policy is both current and reflective of the verified BAA status.

The completion of this upsert action triggers the publication of a policy event to the messaging pipeline. This messaging pipeline acts as a conduit, delivering the policy event to the various consumers, which are different components within the Atlassian ecosystem that need to be aware of the HIPAA compliance status and enforce the relevant policies for the product.

ℹ️ The journey to this solution was not just about improving a process; it was about setting a standard for how we handle the most sensitive data and about reaffirming our commitment to our customers’ trust!

Celebrating the Impact of Expanded HIPAA Eligibility

Generated via DALL·E 3

Our efforts culminated in the successful launch of the HIPAA contract signing and tagging experience. The solution was well-received from day one, with hundreds of customers self-initiating BAA signing in just the first-month post-launch without much GTM push. 🙌

The expansion of HIPAA eligibility has had a profound impact. Feedback from our customers has been overwhelmingly positive, with many expressing gratitude for the improved flexibility and functionality. Many of these customers were previously deterred by the constraints but are now benefiting from the expanded eligibility. 🎊

✨ This was not just a win for the project but a significant step forward for Atlassian, demonstrating our ability to deliver complex compliance solutions effectively.

The journey of the HIPAA Eligibility Expansion project stands as a testament to what can be achieved with a dedicated team and a clear vision. It was an intense period of growth and learning, and the experience has left an indelible mark on the team. 🎖️
