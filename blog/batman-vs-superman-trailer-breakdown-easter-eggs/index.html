Batman Vs Superman Trailer Breakdown + Easter Eggs!

Read complete Batman Vs Superman trailer breakdown! DIscover bonus easter eggs content which you might have missed before the ultimate showdown next year.

Jatin Kumar Malik

When we saw the first teaser of Batman Vs Superman : Dawn of Justice, we ended up wanting for more. Well our dream came true with the release of a 4 minute trailer at the San Diego Comic Con. Well, it’s the best thing right now on the internet. I’m sure everyone would have watched the trailer many times.

The trailer is a treat for hardcore comic book fans

There’s hardly anything you can complain about(except asking for more!) So, here’s my complete Batman Vs Superman trailer breakdown:

#1 Superman is hated for the destruction of Metropolis


Superman gets the heat

At the end of Man of Steel,the entire city of Metropolis was destroyed because of the war between Superman and General Zod. Though Superman defeated(and eventually killed) General Zod,he was the one declared guilty by the US Senator for the city’s destruction and people have started to hate him.

#2 Bruce Wayne is angry


Bruce Wayne is angry

In the trailer we found out that Bruce Wayne was there in Metropolis during the war and he witnessed all the destruction. He too concludes that Superman is dangerous and should be stopped.

#3 The Batcave


The Batcave

The Batcave looks much more advanced and cooler than the one we saw in The Dark Knight trilogy. We can see whole lot of tech in the Batcave. We can also witness Bruce training and the new Batsuit which is a whole lot inspired from The Dark Knight Returns series.

#4 The Newspaper cut-out


Is this the Red Hood ?

There are many interesting theories attached to this.Some say it’s the joker because of the crazy handwriting while some say that a certain member of Wayne family (The Red Hood) is responsible for this.

#5 Jeremy Irons as Alfred


Jeremy Irons as Alfred

He looks awesome as our new Alfred. He looks totally different from Michael Caine’s Alfred though. He repeats the line from the first trailer “That’s how it starts. The fever. The rage. That turns good men… cruel.” I think he will bring his character to a whole new level.

#6 Superman surrounded by supporters


Superman supporters

This moment is in total contrast to what we see in the beginning of the trailer. We can see Superman surrounded by people who look like supporters of Superman urging for help.

#7 First look at Lex Luthor


Lex Luthor

We get the first glimpse of the talented Jesse Eisenberg as the Lex Luthor(Surprisingly with hair !). He is seen with the US senator that we see in the first scene. He might have brainwashed her view on the Superman.

#8 Superman reluctantly kneeling before Lex Luthor


Superman kneels before Lex Luthor

This came as a shocker as we see Superman kneeling before Lex. Lex might have used Superman’s weakness a.k.a Kryptonite,else there’s no way Superman gonna kneel before him.

#9 General Zod’s body


General Zod’s body

Looks like Lex got his hands on Zod’s body after he was killed by Superman. Rumours say he might use it to create the deadly creature Doomsday.

#10 A dead Robin ?


A dead Robin ?

We see Bruce Wayne gazing upon what appears to be as Robin’s costume which is covered with a phrase from none other than The Joker. Most probably Joker has already killed Robin. We might see more about this in the Batman solo film.

#11 First look at Diana Prince a.k.a Wonder Woman


Diana Prince

We get our first look of Gal Gadot’s Wonder Woman in her civilian identity,Diana Prince. She’s seen attending a party,most probably a Wayne party.

#12 Soldiers bowing before Superman


Soldiers bowing before Superman

Superman looks more like a supervillain in this scene. Lex Luthor might have used Kryptonite for forcing him to do something bad.

#13 Batman turning on the Bat-Signal


Batman turns on the Bat-Signal

We see a fully armoured Batman turning on the Bat-Signal. He is using it for calling out Superman to fight him and end him in order to protect everybody.

#14 Murder of Thomas Wayne and Martha Wayne


Thomas Wayne and Martha Wayne get murdered

We can also see the murder of Thomas Wayne and Martha Wayne and a young Bruce crying over his dead parents’ body.

#15 Another Bat-Suit ?


New Baitsuit

We see Batman with a new Bat-Suit fighting with soldiers in what appears like a desert. These are the same soldiers that were bowing before Superman in an earlier scene. They might be Lex’s soldiers and Batman squaring off against them in search of Kryptonite.

#16 Lex Luthor in possession of Kryptonite


Lex Luthor with Kryptonite

This confirms that Lex definitely possess Kryptonite and will be using it against Superman in order to weaken him. It will be interesting to see how he’s gonna find it.

#17 The Fight is on…


Batman in action against Superman


Batman taking on Superman


Batman using grapplers


Wonder Woman


Batman and Superman staredown

We see Batman and Superman and squaring off. We see Batman smashing Superman down the roof(which is my favourite scene of the trailer). We can also see Wonder Woman is also involved in the fight. Batman is seeing escaping Superman’s laser with the help of his grapplers. In the end we see Superman tearing off the roof of the Batmobile and both having an epic staredown.

Here is the epic Batman V Superman epic trailer for your viewing pleasure!

DId we missed anything? Let us know your favorite moment in the comment below!
