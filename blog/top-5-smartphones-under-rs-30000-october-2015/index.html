Top 5 smartphones under Rs 30000 | October 2015

Top 5 smartphones under Rs 30000 for October 2015, from the Moto X 2nd Gen and Sony Xperia Z2 to the Nexus 6 and the winner, the OnePlus Two.

Jatin Kumar Malik

Flagship | High-end | Mid-end | Low-end | Entry-level

Today,I’m going to enlist Top 5 smartphones under Rs 30000 for the month of June. I’ve already enlisted the Top 5 smartphones under Rs 20000. Now let’s take a look at this category :

>>Top 5 smartphones under Rs 30000:

#5. Moto X 2nd Gen (32 GB)


Moto X 2nd Gen

Moto X 2nd Gen claims the no.2 position on our list. It is powered by Snapdragon 801 Krait 400 clocked at 2.5 GHz with Adreno 330 GPU. It has 5.2 inch IPS Full HD AMOLED display. It has 2 GB of RAM, which proves to be excellent for multitasking. It has a 13 MP rear camera and a 2 MP front camera. Both cameras support Full HD recording. It has a 2300 mAh battery. It runs on Android 5.0.2. It is priced at Rs. 24999.

Verdict: Very good performance with almost stock Android.. It can run any game you like without any stutters. The display is good. The camera quality is decent. The battery life,though is a bit of a let down.

#4. One Plus One (64 GB)

The 4th rank in our list is secured by One Plus One (64 GB). It is powered by Qualcomm MSM8974AC Snapdragon 801 Quad Core processor clocked at 2.5 GHz and Adreno 330 GPU. It has 3 GB RAM It has a 5.5 inch Full HD display with pixel density of 401 ppi. It has a 13 MP rear camera and 5 MP front facing camera. It has 3120 mAh battery. It runs on Oxygen OS, based on Android 5.0 Lollipop, though you can also run Cyanogen Mod 12s which is officially supported. It supports 4G. It has 64 GB of internal storage which is non expandable. It is priced at Rs. 21999.

Verdict: One Plus One is the best you can get . It is ultra smooth. The ROM is full of features. Also, people who like to flash ROMs are gonna love this device. The CyanogenMod support is icing on the cake. At Rs. 21999, it is the best phone available in the market.

#3. Sony Xperia Z2


Sony Xperia Z2

The no.3 on our list is Sony Xperia Z2. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 clocked at 2.3 GHz. It has 2 GB RAM. It sports a 5.2 inch Full HD IPS display. The phone is rated IP 67 i.e it is dust and water-proof. It has a good 20.7 megapixel rear camera which can also shoot 4K videos. The front camera is 2.2 megapixel. It has a 3200 mAh battery. It runs on latest Android 5.0 Lollipop. It is priced at Rs. 27999.

Verdict : It performs very good. The camera is excellent. But the best thing about this device is it’s battery which performs tremendously. The display is also very crisp.

#2. Google Nexus 6 (32 GB)


Nexus 6

The no.2 spot is claimed by Google Nexus 6. It has a massive 5.96 inch AMOLED capacitive display with resolution of 1440×2560 with pixel density of 493 ppi. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 805 Quad-core 2.7 GHz Krait 450 processor. It has 3 GB RAM. It has a 13 megapixel primary camera with OIS and 2 megapixel front camera. It is available in 32/64 GB variants. It has a 3220 mAh battery. It runs on Android 5.1.1 with guarranteed future updates. The 32 GB variant is priced at Rs. 29999

Verdict: Very powerful device with latest Android. The UI is butter smooth without any lags. It runs all games very smoothly without any lags. Although the display is massive 6 inches, still it fits in hand. The camera is good. The display is also good.

#1. Oneplus Two


Oneplus Two

The winner of this category is the Oneplus Two. It has a 5.5 inch FHD display. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 810 octa core processor with four A53 cores clocked at 1.5 GHz and four A57 cores clocked at 1.8 GHz. It has 4 GB DDR4 RAM. It has a 13 megapixel rear camera with Optical Image Stabilisation and Laser Focus. The front camera is a 5 megapixel shooter. It is available in 16/64 GB variants. It has a fingerprint scanner. It also has USB Type-C support. It has a 3300 mAh battery, which is non-removable. The 16 GB variant is priced at Rs. 22999 and 64 GB model is priced at Rs. 24999.

Verdict : The Oneplus Two dominates this price category. The performance is awesome. The heating issue is also less compared to other devices. The Fingerprint Scanner is very fast. Camera quality is also great. The battery life also seems good. The speaker volume is a bit low compared to the One. Also, there is no support for fast charge.

So,Oneplus Two wins this one. Share your favourite devices in this category.
