Top 5 smartphones under Rs 10000 | October 2015

Discover top 5 smartphones under Rs 10000 for June 2015. You must check these machines before diving in to buy one. We promise to make your choice easier!

Jatin Kumar Malik

Flagship | High-end | Mid-end | Low-end | Entry-level

The past year and first quarter of 2015 has seen some amazing smartphones launch in the market. Brands like Xiaomi, Motorola, Lenovo and Micromax have risen to prominence.The smartphones in this segment now not only comes with competitive specifications, but also promises to give users best possible user experience at a very entry-level price.

Today, on my TOP 5 list, I’m going to enlist the Top 5 smartphones under Rs 10000 (INR Ten thousand) which deserve your hard-earned money. This price range obviously doesn’t comes with flagship’s alike beastly specs, but low-rangers which provide a very good performance and best bang for buck. But since this segment is flooded with (literally) countless options it can get really confusing to choose between them. That’s why I’m here to help you to sort out this confusion and get you the best device in your budget to justify it’s worth.

So Let’s get started then…

>>Top 5 smartphones under Rs 10000:

#5. Xiaomi Redmi 2


Xiaomi Redmi 2

On no.5 on our list is the XIaomi Redmi 2. Xiaomi has made various changes from it’s predecessor, the Redmi 1s. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 SOC clocked at 1.2 GHz. It has 1 GB of RAM. It has a 8 megapixel rear camera and 5 megapixel front camera. It has 8 GB internal memory,which is expandable upto 32 GB. It packs a 2200 mAh battery. It runs Xiaomi’s custom MIUI 6.0 based on Android 4.4.4. It has dual sim with both sims supporting 4G.

Verdict– It is a decent device with good performance. The main drawback is 1 GB of RAM , which makes the phone sometimes laggy, due to the heavy RAM usage by MIUI. But you will definitely like MIUI.

#4. Yu Yunique


Yu Yunique

On 4th spot is Yu Yunique. It is Yu’s 3rd device that has been launched in India. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 SOC clocked at 1.2 GHz. It has 1 GB RAM.It has a 8 megapixel rear camera which can record videos in 1080p and a 2 megapixel front camera. It has 8 GB internal storage, which is expandable upto 32 GB. It has a 2000 mAh battery. It runs on stock Android Lollipop v 5.1.1. It has a dual sim capability with 4G. It is priced at Rs.4999.

Verdict- It is a device with good performance. This phone is total value for money at 5k. The camera quality is also good.

#3. Yu Yuphoria


Micromax Yu Yuphoria

Yu Yuphoria is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 410 SOC clocked at 1.2 GHz. The USP of the YU Yuphoria is that it has a metal frame around the body that makes the phone look much better for its price. It has 2 GB of RAM . It has a 720p 5.0 inch display with 294 ppi pixel density. It has a 8 megapixel rear camera and 5 megapixel front camera. It has 16 GB internal storage with no memory card slot. It has a battery of 2230 mAh, which isn’t tested yet. It also has 4G connectivity. It is priced at Rs. 7000. It has dual sim with both sims support 4G.

Verdict– It’s an eye catcher,courtesy of the metal frame,which is only found in high end devices. The performance is really good. CM offers a lot of customizations. The absence of memory card slot can be a worry.

#2. Yu Yureka Plus


Yu Yureka Plus

The newly launched Yureka Plus is second on our list . It is the successor to the popular Yureka. It has the same Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 processor with 2 GB RAM. But this time around,it has a FULL HD Display compared to the HD display of the original Yureka. Also,the frame is now of metal,just like the Yuphoria. The rear camera is the same 13 megapixel,but this time it has a Sony sensor,which makes the images more crispier than before. It has a 2500 mAh battery. It runs of Cyanogen 12s based on Andrid 5.0.2 Lollipop. It is priced at Rs.8999.

Verdict: It performs very well like it’s predecessor,although games are little laggy compared to the original Yureka due to the FHD. The camera quality is very good. The device looks better due to the metallic frame. Overall,a very good buy at this price.

#1. Lenovo K3 Note


Lenovo K3 Note

The winner of this segment is the Lenovo K3 Note. It is powered by Mediatek MT6752 octa core processor clocked at 1.7 GHz. The GPU is Mali-T760MP2. It has 2 GB of RAM. It has a 5.5 inch Full HD display withe pixel density of 401 ppi. It has a 13 megapixel rear camera and 5 megapixel front camera. It has a 3000mAh battery that will surely be good. It supports 4G. It runs on Android 5.0. It is priced at Rs. 10000 and will be sold on Flipkart through flash sales.

Verdict: This phone claims the 1st spot in our rankings. It has beastly performance but future updates are bit of a worry with MediaTek. It is the cheapest phone which offers FHD display. The camera quality is good. The only thing that can mess up this device is the flash sale model. This is the best phone under Rs. 10000.

So, Lenovo K3 Note dominates the 10k category. So what are your thoughts on this ? Do share with us.
