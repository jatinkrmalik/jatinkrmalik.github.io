Top 5 smartphone under Rs 20000 | October 2015

Here are the Top 5 smartphone under Rs 20000 that you can buy right now. This category is very popular for flagship level performance at a very low price.

Jatin Kumar Malik

Flagship | High-end | Mid-end | Low-end | Entry-level

I already enlisted Top 5 smartphones under Rs 10000 few days back. Now, It’s time to move on to our next category i.e Top 5 smartphones under Rs 20000. This category has made tremendous progress since the launch of Xiaomi Mi 3 (I own it ! But I won’t list it as it doesn’t sell anymore). Many OEMs have started providing flagship devices at this price. This has made this segment very popular and competitive. So here are the Top 5 smartphone under Rs 20000

>>Top 5 smartphones under Rs 20000:

#5. Xiaomi Mi4i (32 GB)


Xiaomi Mi4i

No.5 on our list is Xiaomi Mi4i. Xiaomi has become a very popular in last few months in India. They launched their latest flagship the Xiaomi Mi4i in India last month,calling it the ‘Flagship For India’. It has a 5.0 inch Full HD display with a pixel density of 441 ppi. It is powered by 2nd-Gen Qualcomm MSM8939 Snapdragon 615 Quad-core processor clocked at. 1.7 GHz Cortex-A53 & quad-core 1.1 GHz Cortex-A53 and Adreno 405 as the GPU. It has 2GB of RAM which is enough for good multitasking. It has a 13 MP rear camera which can shoot 1080p videos @ 30fps. The front camera is 5 MP,which proves good for selfies. It has 3120 mAh battery which provides a very good battery life. It has 32 GB of internal storage, with no microSD slot. It runs MIUI 6 based on Android 5.0 Lollipop. It has 2 SIM slots, both supporting 4G sims. It is priced at Rs. 14999.

Verdict: A very good device with overall good performance. Xiaomi also released a firmware update to solve the heating issue. At, Rs. 14999 it’s a very good deal.

#4. Samsung Galaxy J7

Recently launched Samsung Galaxy J7 is no.4 on our list. It sports a 5.5 inch AMOLED HD display with a pixel density of 267 ppi. It is powered by Exynos 7580 octa-core processor clocked at 1.5 Ghz. It has 1.5 GB of RAM. It features a 13 MP rear camera and a 5 MP front camera. It has a 3000 mAh battery. It has 16 GB of nternal storage,which is expandable upto 128 GB. It is also a dual sim with 4G support. It runs on Android 5.1 Lollipop with Samsung’s TouchWiz. It’s priced at Rs. 14999.

Verdict: It is a very powerful device. The octa-core Exynos processor is very good. The UI is very smooth with absolutely no lag. The expandable storage is also a plus. The phone is very sleek. Currently, it’s a Flipkart exclusive.

#3. Moto X Play


Moto X Play

Moto X Play secures 3rd position. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 615 processor. It has a 5.5 inch Full HD display. It has 2 GB RAM. It has a massive 21 megapixel rear camera and 5 megapixel front camera. It has 16/32 GB variants both with expandable storage upto 128 GB. It has a 3630 mAh battery. It is a dual SIM device. It runs on stock Android 5.1.1. The 16 GB is priced at Rs. 18499 and 32 GB is priced at Rs. 19999.

Verdict: It has very good performance with good multi tasking. The rear camera is too good. The battery is also massive. Stock Android provides a good experience. Very good buy at this price.

#2. One Plus One (16 GB)


One Plus One (16 GB)

One Plus recently launched the 16 GB version of their flagship device. It is powered by Qualcomm MSM8974AC Snapdragon 801 Quad Core processor clocked at 2.5 GHz and Adreno 330 GPU. It has 3 GB RAM It has a 5.5 inch Full HD display with pixel density of 401 ppi. It has a 13 MP rear camera and 5 MP front facing camera. It has 3120 mAh battery. It runs on Oxygen OS, based on Android 5.0 Lollipop, though you can also run Cyanogen Mod 12s which is officially supported. It also supports 4G. It is priced at Rs. 18999.

Verdict: One of the best phones out there. Beastly performance and CyanogenMod support makes it a must buy.The rear camera is very good.All this makes it No. 2 on our list.

#1. Asus Zenfone 2 (ZE551ML)


Asus Zenfone 2(ZE551ML)

The top spot on our list is claimed by Asus Zenfone 2 (ZE551ML). It has a 5.5 inch Full HD IPS display with a pixel density of 441 ppi. It is powered by Intel Atom Z3580 Quad core processor clocked at 2.3 GHz with PowerVR G6430. The main highlight of this device is that it has 4 GB of RAM. It provides one of the best multitasking that we have seen in a smartphone. It has a 13 MP rare camera and 5 MP front camera. It has a Li-Po 3000 mAh battery. It has 32 GB of internal storage which is expandable up to 64 GB. It is priced at Rs. 19999.

Verdict: At Rs 17999, Asus Zenfone 2 ZE551ML the best smartphone available in the market. It performs very well and 4 GB of RAM provides another level of multitasking. The camera is also very good. Battery life could have been a little better but that’s ok.

So this completes our list of Top 5 smartphones under Rs. 20000. Asus Zenfone 2 ZE551ML wins this section with it’s amazing performance.
