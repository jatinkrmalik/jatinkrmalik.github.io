Android pissing over Apple in Google maps for REAL! :D

You don't get to see Android pissing over Apple in Google maps everyday. Don't believe me? Check out the post for link to the location! Google-1 Apple-0

Jatin Kumar Malik

HA! YES! It is true.

In Google Maps, Recently discovered is an image of our beloved Bugdroid, the Android Robot pissing over Apple’s logo. Yes! I am not joking. It’s rather uncanny that this sits there in both desktop and mobile app for Google maps unnoticed by Google.

Well, the tech-giant’s rivalry has now reached to a not-so-mature level. If you navigate to the outskirts Rawalpindi, Pakistan, you’ll come across an Android logo urinating shamelessly over Apple’s logo. The exact coordinates of this curious imagery is N 33°30’52.5”, E 73°03’33.2”, you can check it out here.

“Ignorance is bliss”, eh? ;)

Interesting enough, if you switch to “Satellite View”, this slightly offensive(is it?) :P evaporates in thin air. Well it can’t be said for sure whether Google has intentionally closed its eyes on this not-so-courteous gag or some Android fanboy has decided to express his ever-lasting love for Android via editing maps in Google Mapmaker (did you know you can do that?).

Google - 1 Apple - 0 :P
