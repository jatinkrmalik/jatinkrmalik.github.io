🎄Solve Advent of Code 2024 With Me!

Announcing a spoiler-free YouTube video series for Advent of Code 2024 that focuses on how to analyze and approach each puzzle, not just the answers.

Jatin Kumar Malik

It’s that time of the year again!

Last year, I embarked on a thrilling journey solving Advent of Code puzzles and sharing my solutions through blogs on Medium. The response was fantastic, and I was overwhelmed by the support and engagement from all of you.

https://medium.com/@jatinkrmalik/list/advent-of-code-2023-0b0b9a176b38

However, there was one recurring piece of feedback: while many loved the solutions, they wanted more visual insight into how to approach the puzzles, not just the answers.

I heard you loud and clear! This year, I’m taking a completely new direction. I’ve started a spoiler-free video series where we dive deep into the thought process behind solving these puzzles. Together, we’ll uncover the secrets of:

  • Analyzing puzzles step-by-step: Break down the problem into manageable chunks and truly understand it.
  • Developing strategies before writing a single line of code: Plan before you type to maximize efficiency and accuracy.
  • Converting problems into code: Master the art of translating ideas into clean, functional code.

This series isn’t just about solutions, it’s about the journey of learning, coding, and growing together as problem-solvers.

Join me on YouTube

I’m thrilled to invite you to my YouTube channel where the magic happens! The Advent of Code 2024 playlist is your go-to hub for all the videos. You can find it here:

https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PL33VRicG3dcV9JRdzmfYDttlpkc_jmvMc&si=9-jEH8giAyEevpQQ

🎥 My YouTube Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@jatinkrmalik

Whether you’re a seasoned coder or just starting out, these videos are designed to help you approach puzzles systematically and build your problem-solving toolkit.

Advent of Code is more than just a coding challenge. It’s a chance to connect, learn, and celebrate our shared love for puzzles and programming. With this new format, I hope to create a space where we can all grow together, one star at a time.

🌟 Let’s Collect All 50 Stars Together 🌟

How You Can Support the Series 💖

  • Subscribe to the channel to stay updated on new videos.
  • Share the playlist with your friends or colleagues who might enjoy solving these puzzles.
  • Join the discussion in the comments section of the videos to share your own approaches and insights.

Let’s make Advent of Code 2024 our best one yet. See you on YouTube, and happy coding!
