Top 5 smartphones under Rs 40000 | October 2015

Here are the Top 5 smartphones under Rs 40000. This list consists of very powerful devices with awesome camera and beastly performance. Check out the list.

Jatin Kumar Malik

Flagship | High-end | Mid-end | Low-end | Entry-level

Today I’m gonna enlist the Top 5 smartphones under Rs 40000. This list consists of last year flagship devices which are still eye catching and also provide excellent performance. So let’s see which devices make it to the TOP 5 :-

>>Top 5 smartphones under Rs 40000:

#5. Sony Xperia Z3


Sony Xperia Z3

The Sony Xperia Z3 is next on the list. It has a 5.0 inch Full HD LCD display. The device is IP68 certified ( dust proof and water resistant over 1 meter and 30 minutes). It is powerd by Qualcomm Snapdragon 801 clocked at 2.5 GHz. It has a 20.7 megapixel rear camera and 2.2 megapixel front camera. It has 16 GB of internal storage,which is expandable upto 128 GB. It has a 3100 mAh battery. It runs on Android 5.0 Lolipop. It is priced at Rs. 38999.

Verdict: A very good looking device with great performance. Also it is waterproof, hence providing an extra measure of safety. The rear camera is very good. The battery life is also excellent.

#4. HTC ONE E9+


HTC ONE E9+

No.4 on the list is HTC’s E9+. It has a 5.5 inch Quad-HD display. It is powered by Mediatek MT6795M Octa Core processor clocked at 2.0 GHz. It has 3 GB RAM. It has 20 megapixel rear camera and a 4 ultrapixel front camera. It has a 2800 mAh battery. It runs on ANdroid 5.0 Lollipop. It is priced at Rs. 32999.

Verdict : The specs looks good . The camera looks nice. The Mediatek processor can be bit of a worry,though but only time will tell that. The battery life is also great.

#3. Google Nexus 6 (64 GB)


Nexus 6

The no.2 spot is claimed by Google Nexus 6. It has a massive 5.96 inch AMOLED capacitive display with resolution of 1440×2560 with pixel density of 493 ppi. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 805 Quad-core 2.7 GHz Krait 450 processor. It has 3 GB RAM. It has a 13 megapixel primary camera with OIS and 2 megapixel front camera. It is available in 32/64 GB variants. It has a 3220 mAh battery. It runs on Android 5.1.1 with guarranteed future updates. The 64 GB variant at Rs. 39999.

Verdict: The best phone at this price. Very powerful device with latest Android. The UI is butter smooth without any lags. It runs all games very smoothly without any lags. Although the display is massive 6 inches, still it fits in hand. The camera is good. The display is also good.

#2. Samsung Galaxy Note 4


Samsung Galaxy Note 4

The Note 4 is no.2 on our list. It is powered by Qualcomm Snapdragon 805 clocked at 2.7 GHz. It has 3 GB RAM. It has a 5.7 inch Quad-HD AMOLED display. It has a 16 megapixel rear camera with OIS and 3.7 megapixel front camera. It has 32 GB internal storage which is expandable upto 128 GB via micro SD. It has a 3220 mAh battery. It runs on Android 5.0.2. It is priced at Rs. 40999.

Verdict : It is a very powerful device due to it’s Snapdragon 805 processor but the INternational variant is powered by Samsung’s Exynos 5433 chipset which is even more powerful than this. The camera is awesome. Battery life is also great.

#1. Samsung Galaxy S6 (32 GB)


Samsung Galaxy S6

And the winner of this segment is Samsung’s latest flagship,the Samsung Galaxy S6. It features a 5.1 inch Super AMOLED display with resolution of 1440 x 2560. It also has a finger print scanner like it’s predecessor S5,but lacks water proofing. It is powered by Samsung’s Exynos 7420.It is octa core processor with 4 cores of Cortex-A53 clocked at 1.5 GHz and 4 cores of Cortex A-57 clocked at 2.1 GHz. It has 3 GB of RAM. The primary camera is 16 megapixel with OIS and secondary camera is 5 megapixel. Both cameras can shoot 4K videos. It has a 2550 mAh battery. The 32 GB version is priced at Rs. 39999.

Verdict: This phone is a beast. It’s performance is unmatchable. The fingerprint scanner has improved a lot. It has the best camera which provides very good images. The battery life is also great. A must buy.

So Samsung Galaxy S6 wins this segment by huge margin. Tell us about your favourite devices in this price range.
