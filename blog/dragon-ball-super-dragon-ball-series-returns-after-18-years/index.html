'Dragon Ball Super'- Dragon Ball series returns!!

Yes! Everyone's favourite Dragon Ball series is returning this July with "Dragon Ball Super", a new series by original creator Akira Toriyama after 18 years

Jatin Kumar Malik

All the 90’s kids would know what I’m talking about here!


A new series? Yay \o/

Dragon Ball Super!

Yes, everyone’s favourite Dragon Ball series is returning this July after almost 18 years. It is titled ‘Dragon Ball Super’ (International version) & ‘Dragon Ball Cho’ (Japan). The best thing about this new series is that the original creator Akira Toriyama(He wasn’t the part of GT series,and you all know what happened! Hint: Disaster) is back and has already written the plot for the new series. The events of this series takes place after Goku and co. defeated Majin Buu in ‘Dragon Ball Z’. This means that Dragon Ball GT has no place in Dragon Ball Universe anymore(beat that eh?). :P

“I’ve used the word ‘cho’ (super) in so many commercials, games and other media, it is fantastic that the long awaited Dragon Ball Super will finally begin” — Masako Nozawa, Japanese voice actress of Son Goku .

All the characters from the Dragon Ball Z series are expected to make a return for this series.

Image no longer available: Dragon Ball Super- Dragon Ball series

The production has already started. Fuji Television will be producing this series. According to rumours, the main antagonist might be Beerus, popularly known as God of Destruction, from ‘Dragon Ball Z- Battle Of Gods’. The show will be airing in July 2015.
