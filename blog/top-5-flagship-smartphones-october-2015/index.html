Top 5 Smartphones over Rs 40000 | October 2015

Checkout the Top 5 Flagship smartphones in the market as of June 2015. Price no bar, they come with the latest & greatest hardware paired with superb UX.

Jatin Kumar Malik

Flagship| High-end | Mid-end | Low-end | Entry-level

Today, I present to you the Top 5 Smartphones over 40000 in the market with price no bar. This list consists of devices which not only perform exceptionally well but at the same time offer various unique features which can’t be found elsewhere. All the biggest OEMs have come up with their flagship devices this year. So here is the list of top 5 smartphones.

>> Top 5 Smartphones over 40000:

#5. LG G4


LG G4

Next comes the LG G4. It has a fabulous design and the leather back makes it easy to handle. It features a 5.5 inch IPS LCD capacitive display with resolution of 1440 X 2560. It is powered by Qualcomm MSM8992 Snapdragon 808 hexacore processor with two Cortex-A57 cores clocked at 1.82 GHz at four Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 1.44 GHz. It has Adreno 418 GPU. It has 3 GB RAM. It features a 16 megapixel rear camera with OIS and 8 megapixel front camera. It has 32 GB of internal storage which is expandable upto 128 GB. It has a 3000 mAh battery. It runs on latest Android 5.0 Lollipop. It is priced at Rs. 46500.

Verdict*:* One of the best looking flagship device to be released this year. Sadly,same can’t be said about the performance. While many manufactures are using Qualcomm Snapdragon 810,LG opted for Qualcomm Snapdragon 808,which doesn’t performs very good but is not with the par with 810. The camera is one of the best ,especially the 8 megapixel front shooter. The battery life is also good. Overall, a great device but could have been much better.

#4. Sony Xperia Z3+/Z4


Sony Xperia Z4

Sony Xperia Z3+ has a 5.2 inches IPS LCD capacitive display with Full HD resolution. It is IP68 certified – (dust proof and water-resistant over 1.5 meter and 30 minutes). It is powered by Qualcomm MSM8994 Snapdragon 810 with 4 Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 1.5 GHz and 4 Cortex-A57 cores clocked at 2 GHz. It has Adreno 420 GPU. It has 3GB of RAM. It features a 20.7 rear camera and 5.1 front camera. It has 32 GB of internal storage,which is expandable upto 128 GB. It has a 2930 mAh battery. The price is expected to be between Rs.45000-50000.

Verdict: From earlier reports it is seen as it also has overheating issues like other Snapdragon 810 devices. Sony has promised to fix this. Let’s hope. The phone is waterproof like its predecessors. The camera is said to be better. The performance could be better than other 810 devices because i has a Full HD display rather than a Quad HD. More will be revealed as soon as the device is launched.

#3. Apple iPhone 6


iPhone 6

The no. 2 spot is claimed by Apple’s iPhone 6. It is the lightest device in this list. It has a 4.7 inches IPS LCD display with a screen resolution of 750 x 1334 pixels. It features a fingerprint scanner. It is powered by Apple-A8 dual core processor clocked at 1.4 GHz.It is based on ARM-v8. It has PowerVR GX6450 GPU. It has 1 GB of RAM. It has a 8 megapixel rear camera and 1.2 megapixel front camera. It has a 1810 mAh battery. It runs on iOS 8. It comes in 3 variants i.e 16,64 and 128 GB. The 16 GB is priced at Rs.47000, 64 GB is priced at Rs. 53000 and 128 GB is for Rs. 56000.

Verdict: Though the screen size has increased, it’s still easy to hold. The display is good. The performance is very high and runs everything without a single stutter. 1 GB RAM is a drawback as it hinders the multitasking as processes running in the background keep getting closed. The camera is fabulous. The battery life is not good compared to its predecessor. Also, iOS 8 was very buggy upon initial launch but it is now stable.

#2. Samsung Galaxy S6 Edge+


Samsung Galaxy S6 Edge+

And the winner is the Samsung Galaxy S6 Edge+. It has a 5.7 inch AMOLED dual curved edge display with Quad HD resolution. It also features a fingerprint scanner. It has the same processor as the Galaxy S6 i.e Exynos 7420 which has four Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 1.5 GHz and four Cortex-A57 cores clocked at 2.1 GHz. It has 4 GB of RAM. The primary camera is 16 megapixel with OIS and secondary camera is 5 megapixel. Both cameras can shoot 4K videos. It has a 2600 mAh battery. The 32 GB version is priced at Rs. 55999.

Verdict: The Samsung Galaxy S6 Edge+ is one of the best smartphone in the market at this moment. It provides unmatched performance,top notched camera and an innovative curved display which makes it the best smartphone.

#1. Samsung Galaxy Note 5


Samsung Galaxy Note 5

The winner of this round is Samsung Galaxy Note 5. It has a 5.7 inch AMOLED display with Quad HD resolution. It also features a fingerprint scanner. It has the same processor as the Galaxy S6 i.e Exynos 7420 which has four Cortex-A53 cores clocked at 1.5 GHz and four Cortex-A57 cores clocked at 2.1 GHz. It has 4 GB of RAM. The primary camera is 16 megapixel with OIS and secondary camera is 5 megapixel. Both cameras can shoot 4K videos. It has a 2600 mAh battery. The 32 GB version is priced at Rs. 53999.

Verdict : This is currently the best smartphone available. It has the best display, the best performance and the best camera. The new S- Pen also has added functions. You can’t get anything better than this.

So, Samsung Galaxy Note 5 is the best smartphone for the month of October.
